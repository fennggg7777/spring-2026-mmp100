Animation Project

这是一个从紧张到解脱的吐司动画，用 Adobe Illustrator 和 After Effects 制作。两片吐司因为害怕被烤焦而逃跑，动画使用了位置、缩放和不透明度三种变换属性。

This is a toast animation expressing a shift from nervousness to relief, made with Adobe Illustrator and After Effects. Two pieces of toast run away because they are afraid of being burned. The animation uses position, scale, and opacity.

-吐司：ToT 我居然没糊-
-Toast: ToT I'm not burnt after all.-
